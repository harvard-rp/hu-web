## House reference register

The [Harvard College Dean of Students Office](https://dso.college.harvard.edu/houses) and [Harvard College residential life reference](https://college.harvard.edu/student-life/student-stories/housing-day-2024-first-year-pov) establish the names and River/Quad groupings. These are real-world sources with a separate role from the server's RP descriptions.

All twelve shields were located in the [Wikimedia Commons residential college shield catalogue](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Harvard_Residential_Colleges_Shields) and on the Dean of Students site. Harvard specifically [identifies House shields as design marks](https://trademark.harvard.edu/faq) and [requires review and approval of third-party use of its insignia](https://trademark.harvard.edu/frequently-asked-questions-use-of-harvards-name). Wikimedia artwork licenses apply to the illustrations. The project has no confirmed permission from Harvard to publish the insignia. The files below are reference links only.

## Roleplay catalogues

The HU-006C brief supplies the profiles of the twelve Houses for the RP server. The in-app summaries are original and describe the RP characters. The brief also supplies fifteen majors and thirty-two confirmed club names, consolidated from the roster. The app lets a student propose another major.

The five club categories are provisional navigation labels. BDE has ten places. The other confirmed clubs have no communicated cap. Natation is a single entry with leadership type pending. Club de droit is shown as an unconfirmed thirty-third entry and is omitted from the form choices. The brief gives names only for Équitation, Danse, Radio, Débats, Urbex, Aventure and Automobile. The app leaves their descriptions empty. All numeric rosters and named club leads in the app are fictional.

## Character worksheet

Reference: [Original Harvard University RP character worksheet](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Znv224J7nOw4aP1lrOC1RcGSMV0h9RNafV3j3ruS8SM/edit), credited to `@_junelys.exe`. Reuse of its layout or wording requires permission. The demo uses an original form and records the worksheet's five-line guidance for personality and appearance and ten-line guidance for story without imposing a character-count conversion.

All character records, social activity, ranking points, announcements and events are synthetic. The public prototype is independent of Harvard University.
